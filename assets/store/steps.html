PLIXTEAM

Movie night in three steps

1

Point to a server

Self-host the tiny signalling server, then paste wss://your-host/ws into the extension once.

2

Start a party

Open any Netflix title, pick a room code, and share it with the person you want to watch with.

3

Watch and talk

Playback stays in sync while you see and hear each other in the built-in video call.
